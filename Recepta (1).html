How It Works

An AI-powered research loop that keeps your scientists in charge of every decision.

  1. Step 1

    Connect data

    Bring in assay results, compound libraries, literature and in-house records.

  2. Step 2

    Model

    Apply AI and computational chemistry models to the question at hand.

  3. Step 3

    Prioritize

    Rank candidate molecules and delivery options, with the reasoning visible.

  4. Step 4

    Validate

    Your scientists test the top candidates in the lab and confirm what holds up.

  5. Step 5

    Learn

    Lab results return to the models so the next cycle starts better informed.

Feedback loop. Results from step 4 flow back into step 2, so each cycle builds on the last.
